PRIOR AUTHORIZATION CONTINUITY POLICY

PURPOSE

This policy establishes the standards by which Acme Health Plan
will honor prior authorization decisions made by other payers when
new members transition to our plan, in accordance with applicable
CMS regulations and contractual obligations.

SCOPE

Applies to all Medicare Advantage members transitioning from another
plan to Acme Health Plan, and to all internal staff involved in
prior authorization processing, claims adjudication, and member
services.

§ 4.1 — General Continuity Standard

When a new member enrolls in Acme Health Plan, the plan will accept
existing prior authorization decisions made by the prior plan for
ongoing treatments, subject to the limits below.

§ 4.2 — Continuity Window

Acme Health Plan honors prior plan PA decisions for thirty (30) days
following the member's effective enrollment date. After 30 days, the
member's treating provider must submit a new PA request under Acme's
standard PA criteria.

§ 4.3 — Exceptions

This 30-day continuity window does not apply where:
  (a) the prior PA was for a service Acme does not cover;
  (b) the prior PA was based on materially different information;
  (c) the member's clinical status has changed substantively.

§ 4.4 — Documentation

Member services must document receipt of prior plan PA records in
the member's record. Continuity-of-care decisions must be tracked
for compliance reporting.

§ 4.5 — Reporting

This policy is reviewed annually by the Compliance Committee.
Operational metrics are reported to the Compliance Committee on
an annual basis.
