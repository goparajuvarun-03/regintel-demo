CMS-MLN-7521 · MEDICARE CLAIMS TIMELY FILING AND ADJUDICATION STANDARDS
Issuing body: Centers for Medicare & Medicaid Services (CMS), Medicare Learning Network
Regulation ID: CMS-MLN-7521
Effective date: 2026-03-01
Status: Final guidance (binding)
Publication: MLN Matters Number MM-7521, 42 CFR § 422.520 update


§ 1.0 — Purpose

This guidance establishes minimum standards for claims timely filing
deadlines and adjudication turnaround times for Medicare Advantage
organizations. The guidance updates the previously issued MLN-6113
timing schedule and supersedes any conflicting plan-level standards
that result in shorter turnaround times for enrollees or providers.


§ 2.0 — Scope

This guidance applies to all Medicare Advantage organizations
processing Part C claims, including in-network and out-of-network
provider submissions, member-submitted reimbursement claims, and
coordination-of-benefits claims with other primary payers.


§ 3.0 — Definitions

(a) "Clean claim" means a claim that has no defect, impropriety,
incomplete documentation, or particular circumstance requiring
special treatment that prevents timely payment from being made on
the claim.

(b) "Adjudication" means the process by which a Medicare Advantage
organization determines whether to pay or deny a submitted claim,
including all medical-necessity review, eligibility verification,
and coverage determination steps.

(c) "Notice of Adverse Benefit Determination" means a written notice
issued to a member when a claim is denied in whole or in part, in a
form meeting the requirements of 42 CFR § 422.568.


§ 4.0 — Timely Filing Deadlines

§ 4.1 — Provider-Submitted Claims

In-network providers shall submit claims for services rendered no
later than ONE HUNDRED EIGHTY (180) calendar days from the date of
service. Out-of-network providers shall submit claims no later than
THREE HUNDRED SIXTY-FIVE (365) calendar days from the date of
service.

§ 4.2 — Member-Submitted Reimbursement Claims

A member submitting a claim for reimbursement for out-of-pocket
expenses shall do so no later than THREE HUNDRED SIXTY-FIVE (365)
calendar days from the date of service. The Medicare Advantage
organization shall not deny a member-submitted claim on timeliness
grounds where the member can demonstrate good cause for late
submission, including but not limited to medical incapacity, postal
delivery failure, or natural disaster.

§ 4.3 — Coordination-of-Benefits Claims

Claims requiring coordination of benefits with another primary payer
shall not be denied on timely-filing grounds before the primary
payer has issued an explanation of benefits, plus ninety (90)
additional days.


§ 5.0 — Adjudication Timeliness

§ 5.1 — Standard Clean-Claim Adjudication

A Medicare Advantage organization shall adjudicate a clean claim
within THIRTY (30) calendar days of receipt. Adjudication includes
issuance of payment for approved claims and issuance of a Notice of
Adverse Benefit Determination for denied claims.

§ 5.2 — Non-Clean-Claim Adjudication

Where a submitted claim is not a clean claim, the organization shall
issue a written request for additional information within fifteen
(15) calendar days of receipt. Once the requested information is
received, the thirty (30) day adjudication clock resumes.

§ 5.3 — Expedited Adjudication

For claims involving emergency services or post-stabilization care,
the organization shall adjudicate the claim within FIFTEEN (15)
calendar days of receipt. Members and providers shall have access
to an expedited appeals process under 42 CFR § 422.584.


§ 6.0 — Interest Accrual

§ 6.1 — Mandatory Interest on Late Payments

Where a Medicare Advantage organization fails to adjudicate a clean
claim within the thirty (30) day window, the organization shall pay
the provider or member interest on the unpaid amount, calculated at
the rate published by the U.S. Department of the Treasury, accruing
daily from day thirty-one (31) forward.

§ 6.2 — Posting Requirement

The interest calculation methodology and the current rate shall be
posted on the Medicare Advantage organization's public website and
shall be communicated to providers upon enrollment in the network.


§ 7.0 — Reporting

§ 7.1 — Monthly Claims-Aging Report

Each Medicare Advantage organization shall submit a claims-aging
report to CMS no later than the tenth (10th) calendar day of each
month, reflecting the prior month's claims processing. The report
shall contain:
  (i) total claims received;
  (ii) total claims adjudicated within thirty (30) days;
  (iii) total claims pending over thirty (30) days, broken down
       by aging bucket (31-60, 61-90, 91-120, >120 days);
  (iv) total interest paid for late adjudications;
  (v) total appeals filed and dispositions.

§ 7.2 — Public Performance Disclosure

The percentage of clean claims adjudicated within thirty (30) days
shall be published on the Medicare Advantage organization's public
website, updated quarterly, and shall be considered Medicare Star
Rating input data per Star Rating measure C36 (effective rating
year 2027).


§ 8.0 — Enforcement

Failure to comply with this guidance may result in:
  (a) civil monetary penalty under 42 CFR § 422.752 up to ten
      thousand dollars ($10,000) per occurrence;
  (b) downward adjustment of Star Ratings under Star Rating
      measure C36;
  (c) network-provider grievance escalation to CMS Region office;
  (d) corrective action plan with weekly aging reports for not
      less than six (6) months.
