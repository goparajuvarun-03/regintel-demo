INTERNAL SOP — SOP-CLM-014

CLAIMS ADJUDICATION STANDARD OPERATING PROCEDURE
Owner: Director of Claims Operations
Version: 2.4
Last Reviewed: March 1, 2025

This is SYNTHETIC SAMPLE CONTENT for demonstration purposes only.

PURPOSE

This SOP defines the workflow for adjudicating professional and
institutional claims for Medicare Advantage members.

WORKFLOW STEPS

Step 1: Claim receipt and validation
  - Validate member eligibility on date of service
  - Validate provider participation and credentialing status
  - Validate basic claim format (HIPAA 837 standards)

Step 2: Benefit verification
  - Look up member's benefit plan
  - Verify service is covered under member's plan
  - Apply any applicable benefit limitations

Step 3: Coding validation
  - Validate ICD-10-CM and CPT/HCPCS codes
  - Apply NCCI edits and medical necessity rules
  - Flag claims with unspecified diagnosis codes for review

Step 4: Prior authorization check
  - Look up active PA records for the member and service
  - If PA found and valid, mark claim as PA-approved
  - If no PA found and PA required, deny pending PA submission

Step 5: Pricing
  - Apply contracted rates from provider contract module
  - Apply applicable cost-sharing (copay, coinsurance, deductible)
  - Calculate plan paid amount

Step 6: Final adjudication and remittance
  - Issue payment or denial determination
  - Generate remittance advice (835)
  - Update claim history

KNOWN GAPS

This SOP does NOT currently include a transition-flag decision step
to identify newly enrolled members and trigger continuity-of-care
processing. Claims for transitioning members are processed under
standard PA rules without honoring prior-plan decisions.

REVIEW

This SOP is reviewed semi-annually. Last reviewed March 2025.
