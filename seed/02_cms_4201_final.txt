This is SYNTHETIC SAMPLE CONTENT for demonstration purposes only.
Do not use for compliance decisions.

§ 422.138(a) — Continuity of Care for Transitioning Members (REVISED)

Plans must continue to honor prior authorization decisions made by
another organization for a minimum of 90 days from the date the
enrollee transitioned plans, and for the full duration of any
clinically established treatment plan, whichever is longer.

This represents a CHANGE from the proposed 30-day window. CMS
determined the longer window better protects enrollee continuity of
care during transitions and reduces administrative burden on
treating providers.

§ 422.138(b) — Denial Notice Requirements (NEW)

Plans must issue a written denial notice that includes appeal rights
and is delivered within timeframes consistent with adverse-determination
notice rules under § 422.568. The notice must specifically state:

  (1) the reason for the denial,
  (2) the enrollee's appeal rights including expedited appeal options,
  (3) information about external review.

§ 422.138(c) — Quarterly Compliance Reporting (NEW)

Plans must submit quarterly reports to CMS attesting to compliance
with the continuity-of-care provisions of this section. Reports must
use the prescribed reporting template and be submitted within 30 days
of quarter-end.

§ 422.138(d) — System Integration Requirements

Plans must implement systems capable of receiving and processing
prior authorization records from external Medicare Advantage
organizations. Manual workarounds are permitted during a transition
period but must be replaced by automated processes within 12 months
of the effective date.

REGULATORY IMPACT

CMS estimates this final rule will affect approximately 30 million
Medicare Advantage enrollees. The expanded 90-day continuity window,
new notice requirements, and quarterly reporting obligations
represent significant compliance and operational changes for plans.

ENFORCEMENT

CMP penalties under 42 CFR § 422.752 may apply for violations of
the continuity-of-care provisions. CMS will conduct targeted audits
of plan compliance during 2026.
