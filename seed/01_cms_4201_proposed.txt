§ 422.138(a) — Continuity of Care for Transitioning Members

Plans must honor prior authorization decisions made by another
organization for at least 30 days from the date the enrollee
transitioned plans. During this 30-day period, the receiving plan
may not require new prior authorization for active treatment.

§ 422.138(b) — Notice Requirements

Plans should provide notice of any change in coverage status to
the enrollee within reasonable timeframes consistent with existing
notice provisions under § 422.568.

§ 422.138(c) — Reporting

Plans are encouraged to track and self-report compliance with
continuity-of-care provisions on an annual basis.

REGULATORY IMPACT

CMS estimates this rule will affect approximately 30 million
Medicare Advantage enrollees and require updates to plan
operations, member-facing communications, and IT systems
supporting prior authorization workflows.
