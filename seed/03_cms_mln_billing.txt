SAMPLE — CMS-MLN-12345 (MEDICARE LEARNING NETWORK ARTICLE)

ICD-10-CM VALIDATION EDITS FOR PROFESSIONAL CLAIMS

Issuing Body: Centers for Medicare & Medicaid Services (CMS)
Document ID: CMS-MLN-12345
Status: MLN Matters Article
Effective Date: July 1, 2026

This is SYNTHETIC SAMPLE CONTENT for demonstration purposes only.

OVERVIEW

Effective July 1, 2026, CMS will implement new ICD-10-CM validation
edits for professional claims submitted to Medicare contractors. These
edits will reject claims where the diagnosis code does not meet
specificity requirements for the reported procedure.

KEY CHANGES

1. Diagnosis-procedure pairing edits will be enforced at the claim
   level prior to adjudication.

2. Claims with unspecified diagnosis codes (e.g., codes ending in .9)
   will be rejected when used with procedure codes that require
   higher specificity.

3. Providers will receive remittance advice codes indicating the
   specific edit triggered.

PROVIDER IMPACT

Providers should review their charge capture and coding workflows to
ensure diagnosis codes are documented with the highest level of
specificity. Practice management systems may need updates to flag
unspecified codes prior to claim submission.

PAYER IMPACT

Medicare Advantage plans that mirror CMS edits will need to update
their claims processing systems to reflect these new validation
rules.
