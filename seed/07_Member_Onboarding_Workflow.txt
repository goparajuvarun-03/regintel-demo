§ 1.0 — Purpose

This workflow defines the end-to-end steps for onboarding a newly
enrolled Acme Health Plan Medicare Advantage member, from receipt
of the enrollment file to the member's first claim being processed.
It coordinates the Member Services, Care Management, Pharmacy
Benefits, and Provider Network teams.


§ 2.0 — Scope

This workflow applies to all new Medicare Advantage enrollees in
Acme Health Plan, including AEP, OEP, IEP, ICEP, and SEP enrollment
periods. It governs the first thirty (30) days following the
effective enrollment date.


§ 3.0 — Workflow Steps

STEP 1 — Enrollment File Receipt (Day 0)
Member Services receives the daily enrollment file from CMS. The
file is loaded into MEMBER-DB and triggers downstream onboarding
events. Identification of transitioning members (those previously
enrolled with another insurer) is based on the Prior Insurer Code
field in the CMS file.

STEP 2 — Welcome Packet Generation (Day 1-3)
The system generates a welcome packet containing the member ID
card, summary of benefits, and printed provider directory. The
packet is mailed by Member Communications within five (5) business
days of enrollment file receipt.

STEP 3 — Primary Care Provider (PCP) Selection (Day 1-30)
The member is prompted to select a PCP via the member portal,
member app, or Member Services line. If no selection is made by
day thirty (30), the system auto-assigns a PCP based on member
address and current network availability.

STEP 4 — Prior Authorization Continuity Check (Day 1-7)
For transitioning members, Member Services attempts to obtain the
prior insurer's PA records via the Member Services line. Members
are advised to provide their prior insurer's PA approval letters
within fifteen (15) days of enrollment. Records received are
forwarded to Utilization Management for review under the Prior
Authorization Continuity Policy (ACME-UM-POL-014).

STEP 5 — Care Management Outreach (Day 7-21)
Care Management performs a risk stratification on the new member
using claims history (when available) and self-reported health
data. High-risk members (HCC score above 1.5) receive a Care
Manager assignment within fourteen (14) days. Medium-risk members
receive a Health Risk Assessment outreach call within twenty-one
(21) days.

STEP 6 — Pharmacy Benefits Verification (Day 1-14)
Pharmacy Benefits checks for active prescription drug PAs from the
prior insurer. Where active PAs exist for non-formulary drugs,
Pharmacy Benefits processes a transition fill of up to thirty (30)
days while the member's clinician submits a formulary exception.

STEP 7 — First Claim Processing (Variable, typically Day 14-30)
The first claim submitted on behalf of the member triggers a soft
flag in ACME-CLAIMS-CORE. The flag prompts a manual review for
appropriate application of continuity-of-care benefits.


§ 4.0 — Documentation Requirements

§ 4.1 — Member Files

For each new member, Member Services creates a member file
containing:
  (i) the original CMS enrollment record;
  (ii) any prior insurer PA records provided;
  (iii) welcome packet acknowledgement (if returned);
  (iv) PCP selection record.

§ 4.2 — Cross-System Audit Trail

Each step in the workflow creates an audit-log entry in the
member's master record, capturing system, user, timestamp, and
action.


§ 5.0 — Service Levels

  - Welcome packet mailing: within ten (10) business days of
    enrollment.
  - PA continuity review: within fifteen (15) days of receipt
    of prior insurer records.
  - High-risk Care Manager assignment: within fourteen (14) days
    of enrollment.
  - Transition fill processing: within forty-eight (48) hours of
    prescription submission.


§ 6.0 — Reporting

§ 6.1 — Monthly Onboarding Metrics

Member Services publishes a monthly onboarding metrics dashboard
covering:
  - total new enrollments;
  - PCP selection rate by Day 30;
  - PA continuity reviews completed;
  - HRA completion rate;
  - transition fills processed.

§ 6.2 — Exception Tracking

Onboarding exceptions (delays, missing data, member complaints)
are tracked in the Exception Log and reviewed weekly by the Member
Services Operations Lead.


§ 7.0 — Roles and Responsibilities

  - Member Services: enrollment file processing, welcome packet,
    PCP selection support.
  - Care Management: risk stratification, Care Manager assignment.
  - Pharmacy Benefits: PA continuity, transition fills.
  - Utilization Management: clinical review of prior insurer PAs.
  - Provider Network Operations: directory inclusion, network
    adequacy verification for member service area.


KNOWN GAPS (internal note, not part of workflow)

  - STEP 1: workflow lacks a transition-flag decision step at file
    intake. Currently all transitioning members receive the same
    handling regardless of clinical complexity or prior PA volume.
  - STEP 2: printed directory may not reflect verified provider
    data; pending alignment with the Provider Network Management
    SOP refresh-cadence update.
  - STEP 4: fifteen-day window for prior PA documentation is tight
    given CMS-4201 continuity-window requirements; under review.
  - STEP 7: claims continuity flag is a soft flag only; system does
    not automatically apply continuity rules under CMS-4201.
  - § 5.0: ten-business-day directory mailing exceeds the CMS-2439
    directory-currency expectations for new enrollees.
